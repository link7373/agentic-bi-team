# Request Log

> Created by Colin Beck — https://www.linkedin.com/in/beckcolin/

> Every request the team took on, what decision it served, and what happened to it.
> Written by `/triage`. Read it before starting anything that feels familiar — the
> single most common waste in a BI team is answering the same question twice.
>
> This is also the only honest record of what the team spends its time on. Reviewed
> monthly (see `/triage` → "Reviewing the log"), the recurring rows are the best
> available guide to what should become a mart or a dashboard.
